Henxels (pronounced "henk-sells") is named after the Finnish word *henkselit* (suspenders) — external straps that hold everything together and provide structure you'd otherwise lack.

Like suspenders on trousers, henxels sits outside your project's content and gives it form. It's not the substance of your project; it's the structure that lets the substance hold its shape.

Why this matters:
- **Distinctive** — zero collisions with existing tools
- **Memorable** — unusual enough to stick, pronounceable enough to use
- **Metaphorically apt** — external frame enabling internal freedom

Pronunciation: /ˈhɛŋk.sɛls/ — rhymes with "sink sells".
